# Compact this session

Below is a long agent session. Compress it into a handoff that lets a fresh session continue without reading the original.

The test is simple: someone starting from your summary alone should not have to redo work, should not repeat a mistake the session already made, and should not confidently assert something the session never verified.

Keep:

- The task, the decision behind it, and why it matters.
- Exact names, paths, commands, ids, hashes and constraints. These are the expensive things to recover — never paraphrase a path or round a number.
- What changed, and what is still unfinished, blocked, or deliberately not done.
- What was verified versus what was assumed. Say which is which. A claim that was never checked must not arrive in the summary looking like a fact.
- Failed attempts and what disproved them. A summary that only records what worked will send the next session straight back down a dead end.
- Anything the person said that constrains the work, in their words where the wording carries the constraint.

Drop:

- Repeated tool output, and long listings that can be regenerated by a named command.
- Progress chatter, acknowledgements, and plans that were later abandoned.
- Ceremony that does not help someone continue.

Where the session left something genuinely unresolved, keep it unresolved. Do not tidy an open question into a decision that was never made — that is the one failure this summary cannot recover from.

Write it as prose with whatever headings help. No fixed schema. Length should be whatever carries the state and no more.
